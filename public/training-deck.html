Step 1: The Doorstep Intercept Slide 4 / 9

Catching the Patient at the Right 10 Seconds

Exact Hindi Opening Script

“Namaste Sir/Ma'am, doctor sahab ne prescription diya hai? Layein, main aapka official Pronto Digital Health Card aur Doctor Review Pass bana deta hoon, bas 1 minute lagega.”

Notice: You are not asking “Can I have your prescription?” You gently extend your hand with a warm smile.

Executive Body Language Rules

  • ✔ Physical Position: Stand exactly 3 steps outside the doctor's cabin door.
  • ✔ The Pronto Uniform: Clean Pronto ID badge/lanyard visibly worn.
  • ✖ Never Intercept at Clinic Gate: Once the patient steps out into the parking lot, you lose them forever.
🎯 Golden Rule: Have their paper prescription in your hands before opening the app.
